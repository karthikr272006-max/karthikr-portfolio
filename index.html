About

Two ventures, one habit: build the thing.

I'm an AI engineer, entrepreneur, and technology educator based in Bengaluru. My background is a Bachelor's in Computer Applications with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning — but most of what I've learned came from shipping.

I founded EcoEssence to solve a problem I kept noticing: tonnes of temple and market floral waste discarded daily, when that same waste holds usable fragrance compounds. EcoEssence turns post-harvest flowers into eco-friendly perfume, from sourcing through to brand and product strategy.

Alongside that, I run The Hidden Curriculum, where I break down AI, startups, and emerging technology for students who won't find it explained plainly anywhere else — through workshops, content, and structured learning paths like the 30-Day AI Curriculum.

Mission

Make advanced technology and sustainable business practical enough for anyone to act on.

Vision

Projects

Startup

EcoEssence

A sustainable fragrance startup converting post-harvest floral waste into eco-friendly perfume — from sourcing to brand strategy.

  • Founder-led
  • Grant-funded
  • 2025 — Present

EcoEssence started from a simple observation: enormous volumes of floral waste from temples and flower markets go to landfill every day, while carrying real fragrance value.
